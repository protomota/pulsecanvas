PulseCanvas Studio — Privacy Policy

PulseCanvas Studio does not collect or transmit your personal information, music, projects, favorites, visual settings, identifiers or app usage analytics to Protomota. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking or user-file upload services. Favorites and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Radio playback downloads public station playlists and music from radio.pulsecanvas.si, hosted on Cloudflare R2. Music is cached and analyzed on your device for playback and visual response. The hosting service receives routine network request information, including your IP address, to deliver these files. Radio does not upload your files, favorites or visual settings and does not send app usage analytics. Internet access is required for these downloads.

Apple handles App Store and optional diagnostic information under Apple’s privacy practices and your device settings. The App Store edition does not include the Sparkle updater.

If you choose to open our website, send feedback, share information, visit GitHub or use an external support service, those services handle the information you choose to provide under their own privacy practices. A public GitHub report is public; include only information you intend to share. Website analytics, when offered, requires your consent and is separate from the app.

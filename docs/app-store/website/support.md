# PulseCanvas Studio support

Draft for https://pulsecanvas.si/support/. Domain and contact confirmed; page is not published.

PulseCanvas Studio requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. It is free and has no account, subscription, or in-app purchase.

1. Open the app. The included PulseCanvas soundtrack loads paused. Press Play or click Replace audio to choose your own music file.
2. Choose a visualizer or open Library to search effects, browse categories and save favorites. Adjust the palette and visual controls.
3. Open Studio to arrange beat-aligned scenes. Visual settings are shared by scenes using the same visualizer.
4. Open Project settings to choose canvas shape, resolution and frame rate. 4K wide is 3840×2160; phone is 2160×3840; square is 2160×2160.
5. Click Export MP4, choose a destination and wait for the video to finish. The soundtrack is included. Playback volume affects preview listening rather than replacing your source track.
6. Save project to keep visual/timeline settings and a reference to the soundtrack. Projects do not embed audio or custom presets. If the audio moved or access expired, locate it when prompted.

For a blank ProjectM effect, try a native visualizer and send the displayed effect name, app version, macOS version and Mac model with your report. For export problems, include the canvas size/frame rate and exact message. Do not send passwords or private credentials. Music/project files are optional; send them only if you want support to inspect them and have permission to share them.

Contact: [support@pulsecanvas.si](mailto:support@pulsecanvas.si).

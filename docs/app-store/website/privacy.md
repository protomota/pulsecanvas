# PulseCanvas Studio privacy policy

Draft for https://pulsecanvas.si/privacy/; date October 11, 2026. Page is not published.

PulseCanvas Studio processes music and creates videos locally on your Mac. The app does not collect or transmit your music, projects, exported videos, identifiers, usage analytics, or advertising data to Protomota LLC. It has no account system, advertising, or tracking.

When you choose a soundtrack, macOS grants the app access to that file. Saved projects can retain a local file reference and a macOS security-scoped bookmark so you can reopen the soundtrack later. Projects do not embed your audio or source preset files. If you share a project, its local file references/bookmark data may be included; share the audio separately only when you have permission.

The app stores favorites, visual settings, timeline preferences and video format preferences locally. Temporary export files are local and are cleaned up after the export task. You choose where projects and finished MP4 files are saved. Removing the app's local container removes app preferences; separately saved projects/videos remain where you saved them.

The app imports audio files and does not record your microphone. It does not upload files or contact a music-generation service. The included demo track was created by the app's creator using Suno.

Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information under Apple's privacy practices and your device settings. Those services are operated by Apple.

For privacy questions, contact [support@pulsecanvas.si](mailto:support@pulsecanvas.si) or visit https://pulsecanvas.si/support/.

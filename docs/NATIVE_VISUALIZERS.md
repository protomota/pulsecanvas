# PulseCanvas native visualizers (181)

The native catalog follows Brad’s red Finder labels from the Metal effect review. All 181 red-labeled selections are offered; unselected implementation functions have been removed. Permanent IDs remain reserved, and older projects resolve withdrawn styles to retained styles. Review videos and their labels are preserved.

The policy and exact selection are recorded in [METAL_REVIEW.md](METAL_REVIEW.md) and [metal-keeper-selection.json](metal-keeper-selection.json). Original implementations use procedural Metal surfaces; the separate direct-download projectM library and its provenance are unchanged.

## Current visual fixes

Pearl Terrain and Prism Rain use neighboring-object intersections to preserve whole spheres and prisms across repeated cell boundaries. Mandel Bloom has continuously bending, music-driven folds with an integer-power fractal domain. Its bands deform the surface rather than only moving a camera around a rigid shape. Zero Chaos still removes the musical deformation.

Ferrofluid Crown was not red-labeled and is withdrawn from the app. Its smooth-surface rewrite is preserved as a separate development prototype; it is not part of the offered catalog.
